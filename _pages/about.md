My name is Chenxin Zhong. I hold a Master of Science in Mathematics from the University of Regensburg in Germany, which I attended from October 2022 to August 2024. For my master's thesis, titled "Semistability Condition of Adelic Curves," I worked under the supervision of Klaus Künnemann. This research explored the slope estimation of tensor products of Hermitian adelic vector bundles on adelic curves. We found that for a field of characteristic p, the tensor product of semistable Hermitian adelic vector bundles remains semistable.

Prior to my master's studies, I completed my Bachelor of Science in Mathematics at Southern University of Science and Technology in Shenzhen, China, from September 2016 to July 2021. I graduated with an Excellent Thesis award for my work on "The Classification of Quadratic Forms over $\mathbb{Q}$," supervised by Yong Hu. This thesis investigated Galois cohomology on quadratic forms and used the Hasse principle to classify them over $\mathbb{Q}$ by restricting to each $\mathbb{Q}_{p}$.

Now, I am pursing a doctor degree in the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in the field of lattice-based cryptography.
